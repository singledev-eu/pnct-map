# Zoom levels and camera limits

Branch `feat/zoom-levels`, off `main` at `1faf9ad`. It's committed locally and **not pushed, merged or deployed**.

## What changed

**Camera limits**
- **Min zoom:** recomputed from the map size so that mainland Portugal just fits, using the app's existing fit padding (toolbar, legend). It's 6.34 on a 1440×900 desktop and 6.11 on a 390×844 phone, and it's recomputed on every resize and rotation. A map at the country view stays at the country view after rotating; any other view is clamped.
- **maxBounds:** mainland ± 1.5°, widened to the whole viewport at min zoom. MapLibre keeps the *viewport* inside maxBounds and zooms in when it can't. So on a wide desktop (about 12° of longitude visible at min zoom), a plain 1.5° margin would override min zoom. On phones, the plain margin applies.

**Zoom levels**
- **Concelho level** below `minZoom + 2`, which is about one district on screen (8.34 on desktop, 8.11 on a phone).
  - Concelhos are drawn and hit-tested; parishes aren't.
  - A click or tap selects the concelho (panel and `c=` hash) and eases to fit it, at zoom ≥ threshold + 0.5, so it always lands at parish level.
  - The tooltip shows the concelho with "PNCT: all parishes / k of N parishes / no".
- **Parish level** above the threshold: unchanged parish rendering, hover, selection and Idealista links. Concelho outlines stay as thin context lines.
- **Cross-fade:** the concelho and parish fills (plus the hatch, parish lines and concelho highlight) interpolate opacity over threshold ± 0.25 zoom. No layer is hidden or added, so both sources stay loaded and nothing pops in. Hit-testing switches exactly at the threshold.
- **Styling:**
  - Whole-PNCT concelhos use the PNCT-parish style.
  - **Partly PNCT** concelhos have a light teal fill (0.16) plus a diagonal teal hatch (`fill-pattern`, generated in code). That stays distinguishable without relying on colour alone.
  - Not-PNCT concelhos use the current non-PNCT style.
  - The legend switches rows with the level (`body[data-level]`).
- **Focus mode:** at concelho level, not-PNCT concelhos are filtered out of drawing and hit-testing. The concelho filter is the same `pnct_count > 0` expression as the outline layer.
- **Mobile:** at country zoom, taps only ever hit concelhos (about 60 px or more on a phone), so no accidental parish selection.
- **District filter:** it dims concelhos outside the district too.

## Decisions

- **No new preprocessing step.** `scripts/build_topo.py` already dissolves the simplified parishes into a `concelhos` TopoJSON object on `dicofre[:4]`, with `pnct_count` and `parish_count`. It's committed in `web/data/pnct.topo.json` and regenerated by `uv run make web-data`. full/partial/none is derived in the style expressions (`pnct_count == parish_count`, `> 0`), so the data file is **byte-identical** (same content hash `pnct.topo-BHDbcw4T.json`). I rebuilt it to confirm. I only documented the derivation in the `build_topo.py` docstring and the README.
- **Islands:** no change needed. The pipeline reads CAOP *Continente* only, and the IEFP PNCT list (2026-02-04) covers 17 mainland districts with no Azores or Madeira entries. Nothing PNCT becomes unreachable, so there's no inset or jump button.
- **Threshold:** `minZoom + 2` (4× the country-fit scale), relative to the dynamic min zoom, so phones and wide desktops behave alike. +1.5 left parishes about 15 px wide on a phone, too small to tap. +2 still lets the largest concelho (Odemira) fit near the threshold, and the zoom floor covers the rest.
- **Concelho fit:** `fit()` now uses the same unit-tested Mercator maths (`fitZoom`/`fitCenter`) with an `easeTo`, instead of `fitBounds`, so it can enforce a minimum zoom as well as a maximum.

## Tests

| Suite | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| pytest | 34 passed | 34 passed |
| Vitest | 21 passed | 29 passed (+8 camera); link snapshot **0 differences** |
| Playwright e2e | 17 passed | 24 passed, no flaky tests |
| e2e new spec, slow basemap (`E2E_SLOW_BASEMAP_MS=4000`) | – | 7 passed |

The new e2e cases are:
- min zoom fits mainland; wheel and programmatic zoom-out are clamped; maxBounds keep Madrid out of reach;
- min zoom is recomputed on resize to a phone;
- clicking a concelho at country zoom selects it (`c=`, no `f=`) and lands at parish level;
- clicking a parish at parish zoom selects it;
- a phone tap at country zoom selects the concelho, never a parish;
- focus mode at concelho level filters out not-PNCT concelhos;
- in focus mode, a click on a not-PNCT concelho (Gondomar) does nothing.

**Delegation:** the e2e spec was delegated to kiro-cli (spec and log in `.kiro-logs/zoom-e2e*`). It created only the allowed file, and its six tests passed on the first run. I reviewed it and added the Gondomar focus-click case.

| | Baseline (`main`) | `feat/zoom-levels` |
|---|---|---|
| Parishes drawn (`data-parishes-loaded`) | 7,231 ms | 7,274 ms (now waits for both sources) |
| Full idle (`data-ready`) | 12,014 ms | 12,026 ms |
| `pnct.topo.json` | 498.7 KB transfer (1,768 KB raw, 491 KB gzipped) | **unchanged, same file** |

The difference (+43 ms, under 1%) is within run-to-run noise.

## Left for later

- **Glyph ranges not removed.** The Arabic `1536-1791` and `65024-65279` and the Tifinagh `11520-11775` glyphs are still requested, before and after this change. At the phone min zoom, the view extends below 36.6°N (the 60 px bottom padding), which loads the z6 basemap tile with northern Morocco. Options: a smaller phone bottom padding, or label-layer filters in the basemap style.
- **Phone legend:** at country zoom on a 390×844 phone, the 4-row concelho legend covers part of the Algarve. A collapsible legend would fix it.
- **No breadcrumb.** Going back up a level is done by zooming out; the panel's "Show all of …" goes from a parish to its concelho, as before.
- **Phone check:** this hasn't been checked on a real phone yet. The next step is `npm run deploy:preview`, then a phone check, then production.

# Release 0.8.0: towns, whole-country button, housing and income, shortlist

2026-11-03.

**GitHub release:** https://github.com/singledev-eu/pnct-map/releases/tag/v0.8.0 (marked **Latest**)

- **Whole-country button** "All" / "Tudo" under the zoom buttons; on phones the zoom group stays above the half sheet. Report: [01b](night/01b-whole-country.md).
- **Towns:** the 278 concelho seats and 18 district capitals (OpenStreetMap, ODbL), above the basemap labels, with a legend toggle. Edge pointers show an off-screen seat or capital with its distance ("↙ Coruche · 10 km"). Report: [04](night/04-main-cities.md).
- **Concelho card, "Housing and income":** INE median sale price per m² (2025), IRS gross income per taxpayer (2024) and average monthly earnings (2024), each next to Portugal's value. All 278 concelhos have a value for each. Report: [07](night/07-concelho-card-v2.md).
- **Shortlist:** star a parish or concelho; share the list with a link; a shared list never overwrites the receiver's own. Report: [08](night/08-shortlist.md).

Decisions from the review:
- edge pointers and city labels above the basemap labels: kept;
- IMI: left out (no openly licensed source);
- rents and Junta contacts: report only;
- relief (05) and the bearing swing (09): not in the release.

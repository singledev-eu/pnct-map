## Fade thresholds

The country-view zoom is not a constant: it is `minZoom` from `cameraLimits()`, the zoom at which mainland fits the viewport with the fit padding. The concelho→parish level is `levelZoom = minZoom + LEVEL_OFFSET (2)`. The fade is:

- **opacity 0 up to `minZoom + 0.5`**: so a small pinch or scroll at the country view stays clean;
- **linear fade-in over 1.5 zoom levels, full at `minZoom + 2` = `levelZoom`**: overlays reach full strength exactly at the parish level. A concelho click always lands at `levelZoom + 0.5` or deeper, so after a click they are always at full strength.

| Viewport | Country view (`minZoom`) | Fade start | Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone 375×667 | 5.66 | 6.16 | 7.66 |
| Phone 390×844 | 6.11 | 6.61 | 8.11 |
| Desktop 1280×800 | 6.14 | 6.64 | 8.14 |
| Desktop 1440×900 | 6.34 | 6.84 | 8.34 |

The thresholds are recomputed with the camera limits on resize or rotation. The same expressions apply in both themes; only the full-strength RNAP tint differs (0.08 / 0.16, as in 0.4.0). The hint shows while at least one overlay is on and zoom < fade start, i.e. while the overlays are fully invisible.

## Deviations from the prompt

- **Report path:** `docs/reports/0.4.1-report.md`, not `reports/0.4.1-report.md`. The project convention (CLAUDE.md) is `docs/reports/<task>.md`, and there is no top-level `reports/`. The clipboard copy uses this path.
- **Screenshot format:** JPEG (quality 82, 264 KB), not PNG. There is no PNG optimiser on this machine (no pngquant/oxipng), and a JPEG straight from Playwright needs no new dependency. Generating it from the deployed preview (`SHOT_BASE=https://preview.pnct-map.pages.dev/`) gave a byte-identical file.
- **Screenshot place:** Peneda-Gerês (Soajo, Arcos de Valdevez, 92.2% protected), not Serra da Estrela. I tried both: in Serra da Estrela every parish in view is PNCT, so the map was all teal; Gerês shows PNCT next to non-PNCT parishes (Ponte de Lima, Braga), the orange RNAP outline and the Natura hatch.
- **Hint shown only with an overlay on.** With every overlay off there is nothing to zoom in for, so the hint would mislead.
- **Explainer close paths:** besides Esc and outside click/tap, it has a × button (needed on the phone sheet) and closes when focus is tabbed out of it. In that case focus stays where the user tabbed to.
- **Apostrophes and quotes** in the copy are typographic (’, “ ”); the wording is unchanged.
- **Before/after sheets** use 390×844 (the viewport of the existing phone e2e and review shots); the explainer e2e covers 375 px.
- Nothing was delegated to kiro-cli.
- Side finding, not fixed: `web/scripts/protected_shots.mjs` passes a Promise-returning predicate to `waitForFunction`. Playwright treats the Promise as truthy, so its "settled" check returns at once (earlier waits mask it). `readme_shot.mjs` polls with `page.evaluate` instead.

## What to check on the phone

1. **Country view with both overlays on** (https://preview.pnct-map.pages.dev/#pa=rnap,natura): no hatch or outlines; only the PNCT shading. Open the legend and check the hint "Zoom in to see protected areas" is there.
2. **Pinch in slowly:** the overlays should fade in smoothly over about 1.5 levels and the hint should disappear as they begin to show. They should not pop.
3. **Tap a concelho:** after the fly-in, the overlays should be at full strength.
4. **The "?" button:** easy to hit without toggling RNAP by mistake. The bottom sheet should be readable, scroll to the footnote, and close on a tap on the dimmed map, the ×, or a swipe-free tap outside. The legend should still be open afterwards.
5. **Dark mode:** the sheet colours, the "?" ring and the hint contrast.
6. **Rotate to landscape** with the sheet open: it should stay within the screen and scroll.
7. **iOS Safari:** the bottom safe-area padding under the footnote.

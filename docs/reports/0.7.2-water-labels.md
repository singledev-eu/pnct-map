# Water names and river lines (0.7.2)

2026-11-03 · branch `fix/water-labels` (on the 0.7.2 release commit) · from Mike's review of the night-integration preview, item 4.

## The cause, per theme

Checked against the live OpenFreeMap styles and production 0.7.1 (https://pnct.singledev.eu, relief doesn't exist there), at Cota (Viseu, `#f=182310`).

| | Light (positron) | Dark (Dark Matter) |
|---|---|---|
| River and stream names | `waterway_line_label`, from z10, text `hsl(0,0%,66%)` (#a8a8a8) with a 70% white halo: **2.4:1** on its own land, near-white on the PNCT fill | **No waterway label layer at all.** Its only water label, `water_name` (large water bodies), is black text and sits at index 8, among the line layers, so it's below the app's fills too |
| River lines | `waterway`, `hsl(195,17%,78%)` | `waterway`, `rgb(27,27,29)` on `rgb(12,12,12)` land |
| Covered by our layers? | Yes: the app layers go above every non-label basemap layer (`appLayerIndex`), so the rivers are under the PNCT fill (0.5), the selection fill and the 3D veil | Same, plus near-black on near-black |

So it's pre-existing, and not caused by the relief, the slab or task 01's palette. Production at z13 (before): light shows "Ribeiro do Rebentão", "Ribeira do Coito" and "Rio Pisão" as pale grey; dark renders **no** water names.

## The fix

- `web/src/basemap.ts` `waterLabels` (part of `basemapStyle`, so a theme switch gets it too):
  - removes the basemap's water labels;
  - adds three of our own from the same OpenMapTiles source: `pnct-waterway-label` (waterway lines, **minzoom 10** like positron), `pnct-water-name-line` and `pnct-water-name-point`;
  - places them as the lowest labels, above every app layer.
  - Noto Sans Italic, 13 px. Names go through the same `labelField` as every basemap label (PT or EN names from the tiles, Latin only).
- Colours (`WATER_LABEL`):
  - light: text `#0b2c55` on an opaque `#ffffff` halo;
  - dark: text `#d3e7fb` on an opaque `#0c0c0c` halo.
- `web/src/layers.ts` `water-line`: the rivers drawn again from the basemap's `waterway` tiles, above the fills and the veil, **below the slabs** (the 3D invariant holds: no ground line crosses a slab).
  - Width by class: river 0.8 → 3 px, others 0.3 → 1.6 px (z8 → z15). Tunnels are left out.
  - Colour per theme (`PALETTES.water`): light `#24578f`, dark `#8cbbeb`.

## Contrast (`contrast.test.ts`, new cases)

Name colour against each background, WCAG ratio:

| Background | Light `#0b2c55` | Dark `#d3e7fb` |
|---|---|---|
| its own halo | 13.95 | 15.46 |
| land / woods | 12.52 / 10.45 | 15.46 / 12.87 |
| PNCT parish (0.5 fill) | 6.69 | 5.66 |
| veiled land / veiled PNCT neighbour | 12.66 / 7.50 | 15.46 / 12.14 |
| slab tops (PNCT, neutral, pop, neutral pop) | 5.16, 6.63, 7.28, 10.68 | 10.75, 10.66, 9.42, 9.38 |
| slab tops with the protected-area tint | 5.14–6.63 | 10.70–10.78 |

All the rows above are asserted at ≥ 4.5:1 (halo ≥ 7:1).

**Not asserted, where the halo carries the contrast:**
- the flat-mode selected fill (light 1.75, dark 1.58);
- in dark, the parishes of a selected concelho (3.73) and a hovered parish (3.41).

No single colour can pass both on those and on the land, because they're mid- or high-luminance tints. Every glyph sits on its opaque halo, at 14–15:1. The place names behave the same way there.

River lines: ≥ 3:1 (non-text) on land, woods and a PNCT parish, asserted. Light: 6.65 / 5.55 / 3.55. Dark: 9.71 / 8.09 / 3.56.

## Decisions for Mike

1. **Rivers above the veil:** around a 3D selection they stay at full strength while the rest of the map is veiled. The alternative is to put them below the veil, so they dim with everything else.
2. **Light text is very dark navy** (`#0b2c55`): that's needed for 4.5:1 on the light PNCT slab top. A lighter blue (e.g. positron's `#495e91`) reads more "water", but fails on the slab (2.4:1).

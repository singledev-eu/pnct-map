# Release 0.7.2: dark-mode slab labels, protected areas on the slab, water names

2026-11-03.

**GitHub release:** https://github.com/singledev-eu/pnct-map/releases/tag/v0.7.2 (marked **Latest**)

- **Dark-mode slab labels:** place names over a raised slab reach WCAG AA in both themes. The dark slab tops are deep, and the dark veil is stronger (0.75). On a partly-PNCT concelho, its edge and inner rims now rise above the raised PNCT parishes. Report: [01](night/01-dark-slab-labels.md).
- **Protected areas on a 3D selection:** the slab stays opaque, and the selection's protected parts are drawn on its top. Report: [01c](night/01c-protected-on-slabs.md).
- **Water names and rivers:** readable in both themes (dark mode had no river names), with the rivers drawn above the fills and the veil. Report: [water](0.7.2-water-labels.md).
- **Tooling:** screenshot waits cover paint transitions; `make clean` spares hand-made contact data. Report: [03](night/03-tooling-fixes.md).

Decisions from the review: the deep slab with the stronger veil (not brighter labels); rivers above the veil. Phone check passed before the release.
